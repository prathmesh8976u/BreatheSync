Gaur Gopal Das

Gaur Gopal Das ( born 24 December 1973) is an Indian motivational speaker. He is a member of the International Society for Krishna Consciousness (ISKCON). He is author of book life's amazing secrets

Sadhguru

Jagadish "Jaggi" Vasudev (born 3 September, 1957), founder of the Isha Foundation. Since 1992, he has been offering educational and spiritual activities. Author of Inner Engineering and an international Motivativational speaker.

Nicholas James Vujicic

Nicholas James Vujicic (born 4 December 1982) is an Australian-American evangelist and motivational speaker of Serbian descent. He was born with tetra-amelia syndrome, a rare disorder characterized by the absence of arms and legs. He is author of Be the hands and feet

Tony Robbins

Anthony Jay Robbins (born February 29, 1960) is an American author, coach, and motivational speaker. Famous for his seminars and books like Unlimited Power and Awaken the Giant Within.
